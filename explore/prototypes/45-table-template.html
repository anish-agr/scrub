Before you print

  1. Print dialog: paper Letter, scale 100 percent (not "fit to page"), margins none, headers and footers off.
  2. Print tile 1 only first. Measure the 100 mm ruler with a real ruler. It must read 100 mm.
  3. Trim to the crop marks, butt the tiles by their numbers, tape the back. Table origin is the pan axis cross, arm at the bottom of the sheet.
  4. Tape positions for the three slots, return spot and safe spot come from layout.json.

Arm base footprint is drawn as a 100 x 100 mm square, an estimate: measure your clamp and fix it with ?base=W,D in the URL (mm). The pan axis is the layout origin (0, 0). The 400 mm arc is the ~0.4 m reach from the docs, not a tested limit.

This page does not move the arm and has no STOP; it only draws.
