Main loop

One request, end to end, from the repo README. Orange boxes are the product core. Tags on the right name the track each step earns.
Reading the diagram
  1. Phone on speaker calls the Scrub number: "Hemostat." Community Kitchen: hands-free help for a short-staffed clinic.
  2. Photon number takes the call and routes it to voice/. The summary text goes out on the same thread.
  3. ElevenLabs voice agent hears the request and speaks the replies. This is the Photon Best Voice Agent entry.
  4. Gemini in voice/ turns the words into a structured command. Earns Best Use of Gemini API.
  5. vision/ runs in parallel: a MediaPipe hand tracker finds the open hand, and Perceptron perception reads the tray state. Earns Perceptron Perception.
  6. controller/ is the only part that moves the arm. Everything else sends it information.
  7. arm/ with Isaac 0.5 runs the fine-tuned policy from Perceptron. Task difficulty and execution score here for Perceptron Robotics.
  8. SO-101 brings the instrument to the hand handle first and lets go when the clinician pulls or says "got it."
  9. Ledger and dashboard: MongoDB Atlas keeps the record, and the dashboard on Vultr, under our GoDaddy domain, shows it.
04

Pitch angles

One paragraph per panel. Side-track judges can arrive at any time during the 50-minute round, so everyone on the team should know all four.

Community Kitchen judges

Innovation, impact, problem-solving, technical execution

Clinics cannot hire enough assistants. Two out of three dentists who are recruiting say finding one is very or extremely hard, and a surgical tech job stays open about four months. Scrub passes the instrument when the clinician asks out loud, handle first, and lets go when they take it. Scalpels go to a fixed safe spot instead of hand to hand. Returned instruments go back to their slots and Scrub keeps the count. One fine-tuned model runs an operating room, a dental office and a robotics bench.

Say: "The doctor never turns away from the patient."

Perceptron

Creativity, task difficulty, technical execution; short working-demo video

We fine-tuned Isaac 0.5 on our own demonstrations of the handoff and the put-back, nine instruments across three trays. Perceptron perception reads the tray state, grasp check, returns and the count. We trained and tested under bright, normal and dim light because operating lamps and steel throw glare, and we show the success rate for each.

Show: the lighting table, then one live handoff, then one failed trial and what changed.

Photon

Most creative and technically impressive agent; Best Voice Agent

Scrub has a real phone number on Photon. The clinician calls from a phone on speaker and talks to an ElevenLabs voice agent that answers out loud, so it works as a voice agent from the first word. When the case ends, the summary of what went out and what came back arrives as a text on the same thread.

Demo: the judge calls the number, asks for the scissors, and gets them.

DubHacks NEXT

Real problem and user, builder energy, iteration, revenue, drive

The team plan says dental offices first, priced per chair. Every chair needs an assistant handing instruments, and dental offices report they cannot hire one. The same system moves to operating rooms and labs with a new tray and new demonstrations, no new code. Scrub runs on an open-source arm that costs a few hundred dollars, and the software is ready for a medical-grade arm. The weekend shows iteration through the lighting tests and the ten clean full runs.

Lead with the missing assistant and the per-chair price, then the robot.
