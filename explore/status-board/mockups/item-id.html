Decision rule (controller side, before any pick)
1. Position says what should be there. Detect and tag say what is there.
2. All present signals agree: GO, pick from that box.
3. Tag and position agree, detect differs or is under 0.60: GO, log the detect mismatch.
4. Position and detect agree, no tag fitted: GO.
5. Any other disagreement: CHECK TRAY. Do not pick. Scrub says "check the tray".
6. No detection in the box: EMPTY. Request is refused with "I could not find it."
