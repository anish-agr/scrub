    Why not water?

    Water is denser than oil, so it sinks beneath the burning grease and flashes to steam. That steam expands roughly 1,700 times in volume and throws burning oil out of the pan and across the kitchen.

    1,700xsteam expansion when a cup of water hits hot oil

    Never

    • Pour water on it
    • Carry the pan outside
    • Fan it with a towel

    What the arm does

    • Slides a metal lid over the pan from the side
    • Cuts the oxygen, then kills the heat
    • Leaves the lid on until fully cool

    Why the stove US home fire stats

    #1

    Cooking is the leading cause of US home fires.

    TOP

    Unattended cooking is the leading contributing factor in cooking fires.

    Roughly two thirds of cooking fires start with food or grease catching fire.

    Figures follow NFPA home cooking fire reporting. The system is built by a former volunteer firefighter: protect people first. The arm only places a lid and never touches flame by hand. For any large or spreading fire, leave and call 911.
