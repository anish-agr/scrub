Limits used

Datasheet, STS3215 (Feetech datasheet, translated, via Core Electronics; retailer listings agree):

Operating temperature -20 to 60 C. Operating voltage 6 to 12.6 V (4 to 14 V limits listed for the 12 V variant). Stall current about 2.7 A at 12 V, about 2.0 A at 6 V.

ESTIMATE Warn at 55 C (5 C margin under the 60 C rating), alarm at 60 C. The servo's own over-temperature protection register is reported as 70 C by default; I could not confirm that figure, so it is not used.

ESTIMATE Stall flag: position error over 12 deg and load over 70 % for 3 s. Back-to-back means 5 demos with no rest. Cooling curve: time constant 10 min, ambient 24 C.

ESTIMATE Voltage warn within 0.5 V of the 6 and 12.6 V edges. The mock follower runs 12.0 V and the leader 7.4 V; use your kit's real supply.

Where real values come from

Both arms hang on Feetech buses that LeRobot opens (FeetechMotorsBus). The same bus reads that lerobot-record already does can also read, per motor id 1 to 6: Present_Position, Goal_Position, Present_Load, Present_Voltage (0.1 V units), Present_Temperature (whole C), Present_Current.

The follower port holds the 6 follower motors, the leader port the 6 leader motors, so a small side script (or arm/real_arm.py, A6) can serve them as JSON for ?api=. Do not open the same port from two processes during a recording; read through the recorder's own bus object.

Raw load is -1000 to 1000 (0.1 % of max torque); this page shows its magnitude in %. Position and goal are shown in LeRobot degrees (gripper 0 to 100).
